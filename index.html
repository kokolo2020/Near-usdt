Strategies (research rules, not trained AI)

Trend pullback: 10-candle trend above +0.5% and a red candle. Momentum breakout: close above previous 10 closes. Mean reversion: close 1% below 10-candle average. All use +1.2% take profit, −0.8% stop, or 12-candle timeout. One $200 position at a time. Signal on close, execution on next open; slippage and fees included. The last position is liquidated at the final candle close.
